# Facilitator Guide

Review the frozen specification before examining the implementation. Compare tests to numbered requirements, inspect the unedited adversarial result, and check each completion claim against named evidence.

A module passes at 85 points, with at least 12 of 15 points in Testing and Security. Missing frozen specification, missing adversarial evidence, or an unsupported completion claim prevents a pass.

Ask four questions in every review:

1. Did either coding agent change the meaning of a requirement while implementing it?
2. Does the failure path preserve data, authority boundaries, and audit evidence?
3. Can the learner identify what the test environment does not prove?
4. Would the completion claim remain defensible during architecture, security, or audit review?
